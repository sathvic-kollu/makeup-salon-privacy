Privacy Policy

Makeup Salon: Spa & Dress Up (Android, package app.makeupsalon.dressup) · Published by Zoopet · Effective 15 October 2026

Makeup Salon is a game for children. We designed it so that we collect nothing about the people who play it. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for players to share personal information with other people.

What stays on the device

Your settings, Style Stars, the current makeover and the photos saved to the in-game Lookbook are stored only on the device. They are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, and deleting the app deletes them. If a grown-up chooses "Save to Photos", the picture is copied to the device's photo gallery; it never leaves the device unless a grown-up shares it. Sharing opens the device's own share sheet and is behind a grown-up check.

Advertising

The free version shows a few ads, provided by Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as a mediation partner. Both are on Google Play's list of Families self-certified ad networks. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and as from a user under the age of consent, ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and ads are never personalised. The app does not request or use the advertising ID.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these services may receive limited technical information from the device, such as the IP address (used for approximate country), the device model and operating system, app information and how the ad was shown or tapped. They process it under their own policies: Google and Unity.

Ads are never shown when the app starts, over the grown-ups area or during a purchase, and each screen shows at most one ad. Grown-ups can review privacy choices for ads in the app's grown-ups area where the law requires them.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase, available only in the grown-ups area behind a grown-up check. It is processed by Google Play or the Samsung Galaxy Store. We never see payment details; the app only receives whether the purchase is owned.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to delete or correct; uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Parents with any question can contact us at the address below.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load ads and check purchases), vibration (button feedback, can be turned off), and on Android 9 and older only, storage access to save a photo when a grown-up chooses "Save to Photos".
