Privacy Policy

Makeup Salon: Spa & Dress Up (Android, package app.makeupsalon.dressup) · Published by Zoopet · Effective 9 November 2026

Makeup Salon is a makeover game intended for adults (rated 18+ by the publisher on Google Play and the Samsung Galaxy Store). It is not directed to children. We designed it so that we collect nothing about the people who play it. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way for players to share personal information with other people.

What stays on the device

Your settings, Style Stars, the current makeover and the photos saved to the in-game Lookbook are stored only on the device. They are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, and deleting the app deletes them. If you choose "Save to Photos", the picture is copied to the device's photo gallery; it never leaves the device unless you share it. Sharing opens the device's own share sheet and is behind a short check (a simple sum).

Advertising

The free version shows a few ads, provided by Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as a mediation partner. The app applies the strictest ad settings these networks offer: every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment and as from a user under the age of consent, ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and ads are never personalised. The app does not request or use the advertising ID.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these services may receive limited technical information from the device, such as the IP address (used for approximate country), the device model and operating system, app information and how the ad was shown or tapped. They process it under their own policies: Google and Unity.

Ads are never shown when the app starts, over the settings area behind the lock button (labelled "For parents" in the current version) or during a purchase, and each screen shows at most one ad. Where the law requires them, privacy choices for ads can be reviewed in that area.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time purchase, available only in the area behind the lock button and its short check. It is processed by Google Play or the Samsung Galaxy Store. We never see payment details; the app only receives whether the purchase is owned.

Age

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to delete or correct; uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Questions can be sent to the address below.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load ads and check purchases), vibration (button feedback, can be turned off), and on Android 9 and older only, storage access to save a photo when you choose "Save to Photos".
